### Final run (2026-11-08): what it shows

- **Setup.** All 100 songs of `configs/eval_world.yaml` (55 es / 45 en; 88 already in the finished 24,640-song index),
  every variant; `online` and `both` on a seeded sample of 100 queries each (cold cache, LRCLIB paced). Spoken and
  "sung" ASR clips are macOS `say` voices (the sung ones slowed, moved through pitch steps, with vibrato), not
  human singing: real singing is untested and will differ.
- **Songs in the index are found.** Typed chorus / verse / line / misremembered queries of indexed songs put the right
  song first 97 % of the time (TikTok group 99 %), answering in ~50 ms (index) to ~240 ms p50 (index + online).
- **Zero false matches.** No `match` on a wrong song or on any of the 58 non-lyric sentences in any condition.
- **The weak spots** are (1) the synthetic "sung" ASR (WER ~0.5; 62 % top-1 offline), mostly Spanish sung lines that
  Whisper decodes as English; (2) songs that are not in the index (`long_tail`): offline they are rarely found, and
  the online fallback (Deezer lyric-phrase search + LRCLIB) finds about 70 % of typed queries, less for single
  lines and misremembered words, because Deezer's lyric search needs an exact phrase; (3) Spanish is below English
  (more versions, covers and medleys of the same songs compete).
- **Most remaining "uncertain" answers still show the right song first.** Top-1 (89 % index) is well above the
  correct-match rate (82 %): the margin rule abstains on near-ties, many of which are other records of the same
  song (versions, medleys) or single lines that really occur in several songs.

### Thresholds chosen

The automatic fit (false-match rate <= 5 %) proposed accept 0.52 / margin 0: 534 instead of 491 correct matches,
but 24 confident wrong answers (3.6 %), almost all near-ties accepted by the zero margin. The **strict fit was
chosen instead: accept 0.62, margin 0.07** (0 false matches, precision 100 %), with `uncertain` kept at 0.45 so that
unrelated text is not presented as a possible match (see `world_calibration` in `configs/calibration.json`). A
confident wrong answer is worse than an "uncertain" one that lists the right song first.

### Changes after this run

- Version grouping now also recognises decorated titles ("X (Acousique)", "X (Spanish Mix)"), medleys / mashups
  ("A / B / C", "Mashup", "Megamix", "Mix 4", "Halftime") and same-title covers that hold the sung lines verbatim.
  Re-running the `index` condition with the typed variants + negatives (`20261108T064955Z-world-recheck-grouping`)
  under the chosen thresholds: correct matches 335 -> 344 of 396, top-1 unchanged (91 %), false matches 0 -> 0.

### History

- Pilot (2026-11-07, 30 songs, partial index): typed queries of indexed songs 71/71 top-1; online fallback 66 %,
  2.4 s p50 with ~8 LRCLIB requests per query; 3 false matches at the provisional thresholds.
- Since then: early exit when the local index settles a query (index + online p50 2.1 s -> ~0.06-0.3 s), Deezer
  queried with overlapping 7-word lyric windows plus 5-word retries, LRCLIB title search only as a fallback
  (~2 LRCLIB requests per query), an evidence guard against short generic lines, cover / original grouping, and
  Whisper decoding fixes for sung clips (encoder cache, greedy and alternative-language retries).
